Common questions

How do I book a pickup?
Open the app, choose your services (washing, drying, ironing, dry cleaning), set your pickup address and confirm. A driver collects your laundry and you can follow the order live.

How is my order priced?
Your branch weighs and measures the laundry after pickup and sends the final amount to the app for you to confirm before cleaning starts.

How do points, coupons and the weekly raffle work?
Completed orders earn loyalty points. Coupons apply at checkout. The weekly raffle is free; see the raffle rules.

I work for MLFX. How do I get a staff account?
Managers, drivers and admins join through an invite from an MLFX admin. Sign up with the invited email address.

How do I delete my account?
In the app go to Settings, Edit Account, Delete Account. You can also email us to request deletion.
